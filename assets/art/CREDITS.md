# CREDITS – Dorf-Skater / Milch holen (art)

## Artwork
All sprites, tiles, parallax layers, UI and FX in `assets/` are original. Python + Pillow scripts in
`art/src/` draw them procedurally from the 27-colour `art/palette.hex`. No AI-generated, licensed, stock or CC0 art
is used, and there are no brands or logos. The shop sign says "KAUFKISTE", which is made up.
The concept paintings in `art/concepts/` are reference only and do not ship.
